The Mission

Our club brings together outdoor enthusiasts looking to experience Michigan's winter wilderness. From setup strategies, ice safety, to advanced sonar fishing patterns, we welcome rookies and seasoned anglers alike.

Rookies and seasoned anglers welcome

  • Setup strategies

    Shelters, holes and rigs in place before the bite starts.

  • Ice safety

    Test as you go, carry picks, and never fish alone.

  • Sonar fishing patterns

    Reading the flasher to find fish and keep them interested.

Before every trip

Read the ice first

The Michigan DNR is clear that no inch count makes ice safe. Check it yourself with a spud as you walk out, and turn back if anything looks wrong.

  • Clear, with a blue tintStrongest
  • Milky, refrozen snowWeak
  • Snow-coveredTreat as unsafe

Carry every trip: ice picks or claws, a life jacket, and a spud to test ahead of you.
